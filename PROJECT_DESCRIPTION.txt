PROJECT TITLE: CarePoint — Hospital Management System

OBJECTIVE
To develop a user-friendly web application that helps hospital staff manage patient records, doctor information, appointments and billing from one dashboard.

TECHNOLOGIES
Python, Flask, SQLite, HTML5, CSS3 and JavaScript.

MODULES
1. Dashboard — hospital KPIs, recent patient registrations, and appointment overview.
2. Patient Management — register patients, search records, and view patient history.
3. Doctor Management — add doctors, specialties, contact information, rooms and availability.
4. Appointment Management — book appointments and update appointment status.
5. Billing & Payments — create bills, record full or partial payments, and track outstanding balances.
6. Global Search — search patients, doctors and appointments.
7. User Interface — responsive layout, navigation sidebar, notifications and dark mode.
